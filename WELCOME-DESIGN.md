# Welcome Screen

The October 9 redesign replaces the red field and colored placeholder strip on the welcome route only. The rest of the customer flow keeps its existing palette.

Follow the supplied editorial reference: muted sage surround, cream inset surface, centered serif heading with italic emphasis, compact white start prompt, and a stepped row of natural-color photographs. The entire surface remains a native button. Preserve the configured booth name and existing brand-tap admin shortcut.

Desktop heading size: 72px. Mobile: 40px. Short landscape: 44px. Brand uses Barlow Condensed; body and start prompt use Archivo. Five photos on desktop, three on mobile. No letter-spacing adjustments or gradients.

Source photographs are stored locally at public/welcome-*.jpg; source URLs are recorded in public/welcome-friends-source.txt. These are sample photographs, not customer captures.

Verified at 1440x900 and 390x844: photo decoded, no horizontal overflow, keyboard Enter starts layout selection. Build passes. Physical touchscreen not tested.
